# Night-Shift-At-Powers
A Five Nights at Freddys based game where the animatronics are.. Tim Powers..?

## How to play

| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| Q / E | Close or open the left / right door |
| A / D | Turn on the left / right door light |
| Space | Raise or lower the cameras |
| M | Mute |

You can also click all of these on screen, so the game works on phones too.

- **ALPHA** waits until the others leave the stage. He only moves while you are *not* watching him on camera, and he comes to the **right** door.
- **BRAVO** wanders down the west hall to the **left** door.
- **CHARLIE** goes through the restrooms and kitchen to the **right** door. The kitchen camera is audio-only.
- **DELTA** hides behind the curtain in the Cove (CAM 1C). If you go too long without checking the cameras, he sprints down the west hall. Close the left door in time. Each bang on the door costs power.

Doors, lights and cameras all drain power. If the power hits 0%, the lights go out… and you'd better hope it's almost 6 AM.

Beat Nights 1–5 to unlock Night 6. **Custom Night** lets you set each animatronic's difficulty from 0 to 20.
